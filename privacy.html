Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

The Domino File is an animated history channel on YouTube (@TheDominoFile). "The Domino File uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule the channel's own videos.

What the uploader accesses

The uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the channel owner's own YouTube channel and to schedule their publication. It only acts on the account that authorised it. It does not access, collect, store or share data about any other person, and it does not read viewers' data, comments or analytics.

How data is stored

The authorisation token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's. You can revoke the uploader's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers of the channel

If you watch our videos on YouTube, your data is handled by YouTube and Google, not by us. The channel does not run its own tracking, ads, newsletters or accounts.

Google and YouTube policies

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the channel through its YouTube page (About).
